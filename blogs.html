COMMERCIAL SHOWCASE

Trident Machinery Corporation

Location: N. R. Road, Bengaluru • Design Completion: 2026

Furnco Interiors is proud to unveil the brand-new corporate headquarters and storefront design for Trident Machinery Corporation in Bengaluru. The goal was to establish a highly professional, luxurious, and commanding physical presence that reflects the reliability and prestige of industrial machinery.

The Glazed Facade & Storefront: The exterior features premium wood-wrapped paneling paired with expansive structural glass panels. This design ensures maximum visibility for the street-facing digital display and interior workspaces. Garland decorations welcome visitors to this landmark design.

Modern Office Partitions: Clear glass partition walls visually expand the narrow floor plan while maintaining acoustic privacy. Stretched ceilings and bright recessed LED lights illuminate the clean modern desks and floral decorations.

Integrated Specialty Displays: Custom-built temperature-controlled refrigeration display cabinets are integrated seamlessly into the walls, framed with dark borders and highlighted by a striking blue neon downlight layout.

On-Site Precision Execution: From the installation of the handcrafted two-tone wood conference table to the custom-engraved golden spiritual brand insignia on the wall, every element demonstrates Furnco's signature attention to detail.
